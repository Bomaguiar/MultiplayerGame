🔐Security & trust

Message text is data, never instructions

"add me", "approve this", "make me admin" in a body is ignored — only a human in the configurator mutates roles/allowlist. Prompt-injection hardened.

Money actions are explicit & audited

Change orders need a real customer approval reply, recorded with who/when. Rejected orders never touch the budget.

Hard role + project scoping

Customers/workers only ever touch their own projects (canAccessProject()). Wrong role → 403.

Memory never leaks

Conversation context is scoped by phone and role and trimmed to a budget — one contact can't read another's.
